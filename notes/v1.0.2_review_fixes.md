## Review items and dispositions

1. **Bibliography separator.** Added the missing blank line between the Ohana
   et al. entry and Oppenheim \& Lim. The full bibliography was scanned; no
   other missing entry separators were found.
2. **Off-line coherence.** Replaced the contradictory sentence with the
   artifact-backed result: coherence `0.09–0.30`, at or below the
   phase-randomized-surrogate 95% level at four of eight stations, and within
   `0.12` of it at the rest. The claim-map lint now checks the range, the
   four-of-eight count, the maximum excess, and the exact sentence.
3. **Coarse `±1.0` swing.** Kept the paper at `5.6x`. The committed
   `RUNS/2026-10-23-fc15-neon/fc15_bins.json` gives
   `3.2767942481 / 0.5819575731 = 5.6306411318`, which rounds to `5.6` at one
   decimal. The review's `5.74x` is not reproduced by that artifact; the earlier
   `5.7373` was already identified as an arithmetic/prose slip. The exact
   arithmetic is now recorded in the target `CLAIM_MAP.md`.
4. **Fig. 14 caption.** Changed “demonstrating that time-averaging acts as a
   low-pass filter” to “consistent with time-averaging acting as a low-pass
   filter ... a same-level consistency check, not a clean all-level comparison.”
   This matches the body's hedged register.
5. **F-C2 common-phase evidence.** Added
   `figures/24b_spde_common_phase.json` from
   `RUNS/2026-10-22-fc2-unitary/fc2_unitary.json`. The claim-map lint now gates
   `3.66e-2` common-phase divergence, `2.75e-2` uncorrected divergence, `1.33x`,
   cross-spectrum error `2e-16`, and projection `2.2e-15`.
6. **F-C9 channel cutoff units.** Closed in the audit as a documented
   limitation, not a numerical resolution. The paper now states that the
   channel ratio uses the test's fixed filter cutoffs and is an
   order-of-magnitude contrast, not a bandwidth-matched calibration. (v1.0.3
   moved this from a footnote into the body: the footnote split across pages
   and stranded “calibration.” on the next page.) The qualitative near-wall
   localization remains supported.
7. **Cosmetics.** Removed the manuscript filename from the reproducibility
   sentence, set the archive SHA-256 in one copyable `\footnotesize` string
   without `\allowbreak`, and replaced the second “catastrophically” with
   “outright.”
