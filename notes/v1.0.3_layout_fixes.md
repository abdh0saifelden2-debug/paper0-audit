Applied in `/home/manem/Downloads/K1-two-clocks-theory` on top of `239aff9` /

lint and the CI build did not catch. No claim, number, figure, or table value

## Defects and fixes

1. **Table 8 E4 kill condition was clipped at the page edge.** The table used a
   plain `ll` tabular. LaTeX logged an overfull box of `150.6 pt`, and
   `pdftotext -bbox` showed the word `𝑐-depen` ending at `x = 612.17` on a
   612 pt page; the rest of `c-dependent final state` was off-page. The second
   column is now `p{0.60\linewidth}` and the row wraps:
   `... converges with a c-dependent final state`.
2. **The F-C9 footnote split across pages.** The footnote ended page 16 at
   “bandwidth-matched” and its last word, “calibration.”, appeared alone at the
   bottom of page 17. The caveat is now in the body: “The channel ratio uses the
   test’s fixed filter cutoffs; it is an order-of-magnitude contrast, not a
   bandwidth-matched calibration.” This keeps the gated phrase and removes the
   split.
3. **Duplicated coherence text.** The v1.0.2 off-line fix inserted a pre-table
   paragraph with the 12 h, 24 h, and off-line coherence numbers; the existing
   band-resolved paragraph then repeated them and said coherence “collapses to
   the surrogate noise floor,” which is stronger than the verified result. The
   pre-table duplicate (and its duplicate daily-correlation range) was removed;
   the band-resolved paragraph now carries the exact gated sentence:
   `0.09--0.30: at or below the phase-randomized-surrogate 95\% level at four
   of eight stations, and within 0.12 of it at the rest.` The Fig. 3 caption now
   says “near the surrogate noise floor off-line.”

## New PDF-level gate

`.github/workflows/paper.yml` now installs `poppler-utils` and, after the build:

- extracts the PDF text and flattens line breaks;
- requires `final state` (catches the Table 8 clipping);
- requires the full 64-character archive SHA-256 (catches a clipped digest).

The check passed in the PR build and in the tag build.
